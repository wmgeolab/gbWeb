Global composites (CGAZ)

Global administrative layers at ADM0, ADM1, and ADM2.

CGAZ layers are simplified and gap filled. Disputed areas follow US Department of State definitions. For boundaries as countries represent themselves, use individual country files.

ADM0

Country boundaries

The outer borders of countries and territories.

ADM1

States & provinces

First-level subdivisions, combined into one global layer.

ADM2

Counties & districts

Second-level subdivisions, combined into one global layer.

Global files can be large. Choose GeoPackage for desktop GIS or GeoJSON for web maps. Attribution is required. For reproducible research, use an archived release.
